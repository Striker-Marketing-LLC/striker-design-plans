FAQ

Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What counts as one creative?

One static ad design with 3 platform sizes included. 1 carousel counts as 2 static creatives.

How does "unlimited" work on Scale?

Submit as many requests as you need. Your dedicated team works them in parallel — not one-by-one. Turnaround stays at 24 business hours per creative.

How do revisions work?

Starter and Growth include 2 revision rounds per creative. Scale gets unlimited rounds. After your rounds are used, additional changes count as a new creative in your quota.

Do unused creatives roll over?

No. Your weekly quota resets each Monday. Unused creatives don't carry over to the next week.

What if I need more than my plan allows?

You can upgrade mid-cycle or add rush creatives as add-ons. The difference is prorated with no penalty.

Is turnaround calendar days or business days?

Business hours, Monday through Friday. A request submitted Friday afternoon starts Monday morning.

How does cancellation work?

No contracts required. Just provide 30 days notice to cancel. Payment is handled through a simple subscription.

What does a landing page include?

Up to 8 sections, designed for desktop and mobile in Figma. Longer pages or multi-step funnels count as additional landing pages.
